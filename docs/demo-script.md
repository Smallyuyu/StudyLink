# Demo Script

Target length: 5 to 7 minutes.

1. Open the home page and describe the problem: students need a faster way to find classmates, build study groups, and coordinate learning.
2. Log in as `alice@studylink.test` with password `password`.
3. Show the dashboard, profile fields, skill tags, available times, and goals.
4. Open Courses, choose a course, and show enrollment plus classmates.
5. Create a study group from the course page and explain max members, goals, preferred time, and status.
6. Log out, log in as `ben@studylink.test`, and request to join Alice's group.
7. Log back in as Alice, open the group, approve the request, and show the member list.
8. Open Matches and explain the score formula: same course, overlapping tags, time, goals, and open seats.
9. Open the group chat, send a message, and point out that messages are persisted and also published to a STOMP topic.
10. Close with `docs/diagrams/architecture.md`, the decision log, and the AI collaboration log.
